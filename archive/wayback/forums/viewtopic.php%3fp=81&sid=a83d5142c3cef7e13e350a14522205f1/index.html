I've lately been scanning all my old 35mm slides with an Epson flatbed film scanner I bought - it uses 'ICE' technology to remove dust and damage and the results are extremely impressive.

I thought that it might be possible to obtain similarly impressive results with scanning old movie film, say a foot at a time, and then sequencing the images in Adobe Premiere. It would be very longwinded, but at 4800dpi it should be possible to obtain a resolution acceptable for DVD from an 8mm frame, and get a flickerless transfer too (I don't have access to professional telecine equipment.)

Perhaps I'll give it a shot and post some samples if it proves successful.

BTW, 'ICE' technology doesn't work on Kodachrome!
