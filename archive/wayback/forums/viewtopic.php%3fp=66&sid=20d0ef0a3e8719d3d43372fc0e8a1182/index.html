Hi Kate,
It looks like you have three "Unimotors" (two types) an MST motor, a voltage regulator, rechargeable battery pack and two power transformers. (let me know if I seem to have missed something.) I'll put links to your pictures with the descriptions to help make it easier to identify -- there are also some links to pages on my site with a little more info. Hope this helps:

Motors:
http://www.composerlink.org/bolex/dcp_4047.jpg
This is an MST motor for any H8 or H16 cameras that have a 1:1 drive shaft (but not for earlier cameras.)

http://www.composerlink.org/bolex/dcp_4050.jpg
This is a Unimotor B (or MCE-17B). The other two that look similar appear to be the earlier Unimotor (MC-17) All of them can be used on any H model camera, but the MCE-17B can also be used to power H16 cameras with a 400' magazine attached.

http://www.composerlink.org/bolex/dcp_4051.jpg
This is another Unimotor, but it looks like it may have been modified with a different connection. I'm not familiar with that pin connector, or what might power it.


Power Supplies:
http://www.composerlink.org/bolex/dcp_4052.jpg
This is the voltage regulator you mentioned; it's used to power the Unimotor B when it's attached to a camera using a 400' magazine. It's also designed to be mounted to the side of the camera, underneath the Unimotor.

http://www.composerlink.org/bolex/dcp_4044.jpg
http://www.composerlink.org/bolex/dcp_4045.jpg
BE-22 transformers for the Unimotor. The left side pins are for selecting the voltage, and the right side pins (yellow and green) determine the camera speed (frames per second) and cycles/herz respectively.

http://www.composerlink.org/bolex/dcp_4043.jpg
That's a rechargeable 12 volt battery pack, for use with the MST motor. It looks like you have both cords that originally came with it. (one attaches to the motor, and the other is for plugging into an AC outlet to charge the battery.)
