# Models the price lists name and the archive has no page for

Mining every Bolex price list Pacific Rim holds turned up fifteen models
that Paillard, EPOI or Bolex (U.S.A.) sold and BolexCollector.com never
covered. Each one below has a product number and a dated retail price
already read off the page, so an entry can be written from a primary
source rather than from dealer listings.

This is a shopping list, not a plan. Nothing here has been added.

Two of the fifteen have been done since this file was written, and are
kept at the bottom as the worked examples.

---

## Why the gap exists

Michael's cameras and projectors are strongest on the Sainte-Croix
machines and thin after Eumig bought Bolex in 1969. That is not an
oversight so much as where his interest lay, and the 1970s equipment is
what the surviving price lists cover best. The two sets of gaps are
almost the same gap.

---

## 16mm, from the October 1976 and January 1978 dealer lists

The clearest omissions in the whole archive. Three major production
cameras, all electric drive, all priced twice.

| Model | Product | Oct 1976 | Jan 1978 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H-16 EL, body with battery, charger, cable, filter holder | 41900 | $3,300.00 | $4,918.00 |
| H-16 EL/TV, as above with a TV frame in the finder | 41902 | — | $5,059.00 |
| H-16 EBM, complete, bayonet, with power grip and zoom | 41754 / 41750 | $3,170.00 | $3,499.00 |
| H-16 SBM, bayonet mount, w/o multifocal VF | 41810 | $1,493.00 | $2,500.00 |

Sources: `rl/02211` and `rl/02210`. **Both lists print their two price
columns in opposite orders.** 1976 is Dealer Cost then Suggested Retail;
1978 is Suggested Retail then Dealer Net. The figures above are retail
from both.

## The two already written, as worked examples

- **SP 8** (`/projectors/sp8`). Feb 1972 list, product 2480, $299.50,
  f/1.3 15-30mm zoom. Super8Data independently gives 1972 and the same
  lens, which is two sources agreeing without having seen each other.
- **SM 80 Programmatic** (`/projectors/sm80`). Apr 1979 list, product
  93480, $780.00. Super8Data settles that it is a separate 1977 model and
  not the SP 80 renamed: 240m reel against 180m, both tracks against one.

Both follow the same shape, which is the shape to copy. The price,
product number and wording come from the Bolex document and are stated
as such. Everything else comes from Super8Data, is labelled a collector
database rather than a manufacturer source, and stops well short of the
fine specifications nothing has checked.
