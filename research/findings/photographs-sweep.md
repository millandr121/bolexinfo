# Photographs: what has been found, and where the rest would come from

The site works. What it is short of is pictures, and serial numbers.
This is the state of the first of those, written down mid-sweep so the
next session does not start from nothing.

## Where the pictures come from

Every one of the seventy-five Pacific Rim PDFs is already cached and
read. The method is the same each time: render the page at 600dpi,
crop the figure, save it as webp at 1600px on the long side into
`media/sourced/<section>/<slug>/`, and credit it to the document by
name. `scratchpad/frac.sh <doc> <page> <x0> <y0> <x1> <y1> <out>` does
the cutting, taking the crop as fractions of the page.

**Read the rendered page before assigning a figure to a model.** Two
of these were nearly filed wrong: the colour camera in `02232` had to
have its nameplate read at full resolution to confirm it is the 583
and not the 563 XL, and the `02241` Super 8 page puts a microphone
where the camera looks like it should be.

## Still missing, and whether it can be fixed

**Four cameras.** The H-9 (nothing in the corpus names or shows it: it
is the 9.5mm camera and the American documents ignore it), the 250,
the 5122 and the 564 XL. The last three are late Super 8, named only
in price lists, and price lists carry no illustrations.

**Six projectors**: 501, 510, SM 80, 714, 715, 815. Same reason. The
501 and 510 share a page in `00953` and it is text only; the other
four are named in the 1976, 1979 and April 1979 lists and nowhere
else.

**Forty-one lenses.** Thirty-seven of them appear in no dated
catalogue at all, only in Michael's own pages, and he had no
photographs either: his Wollensak, Meyer and Goerz pages carry a
maker's logo and one generic picture of a lens between the three of
them. Four more (the two fixed-focus Velostigmats, the 4" and the 6"
f/4.5) are listed in the 1946 catalogue but not illustrated in it.

So the remaining lens photographs will not come out of the paper.
They would have to come from Wikimedia Commons, from a museum
collection that licenses openly, or from someone who owns one. The
Commons category holds about 180 Bolex files and almost all are
cameras rather than lenses.

## Next, in order

1. **Accessories.** Eight have no picture and most of them are
   reachable. The prismatic focuser is cut and waiting in
   `research/pending-media/accessories/`; it needs the same
   `photos.json` plumbing the lenses just got, because
   `data/accessory-items/items.json` is regenerated by
   `scripts/extract-accessory-items.mjs` and anything written into it
   by hand is lost on the next run. The Rexofader is on `02241` p7.
   The underwater housing is `02231`, both the diver on the cover and
   the labelled housing on p4. The Stereo is the cover of `01392`. The
   Pellegrini-Piek shutter is the leaflet at the back of `02988`.
   Nothing yet found for the Surefire grip or the Minibolex.
2. **More views of models that only have one.** The sweep so far has
   only filled in pages that had nothing. `00953` alone holds another
   dozen lens figures and the whole accessory range.
3. **The ones only a person can supply.** The four cameras, the six
   projectors and the long tail of lenses need an owner's photograph,
   which is what the suggestion form is for.
