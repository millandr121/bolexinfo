Found by taking the 83 document titles in Pacific Rim's Bolex library and
checking every model name in them against this site's 54 cameras and 17
projectors. The method is crude — it matches names, so a document about a
model we hold under a different name would look like a gap — but these four
were confirmed absent by searching the catalogue directly.

## Confirmed missing

**Bolex 16 Pro / 16 Pro 100** — professional 16mm, c. 1970. Fully
researched from the manufacturer's brochure; see `bolex-16-pro.md`.
Photographs in hand, awaiting provenance.

**Bolex Stereo**, also sold as **Triorama** — 1952 to about 1954. A
single-strip 3-D system for 16mm, not a camera in its own right: prisms and
lenses that record a left and right image inside one standard frame, with
matching projection optics and polarizers. Works only on the **non-reflex**
Bolex. Launch price US$397.50.
**Source:** Film Atlas, <https://www.filmatlas.com/entry/150> — read
2026-11-18. Cite as Hess, Hillary (2024), "Bolex Stereo", in James Layton
(ed.), *Film Atlas*, FIAF / George Eastman Museum.
**Confidence:** probable — one source, a serious institutional one. Pacific
Rim also lists a "Bolex Sterero Instruction Book", which would confirm the
specifications independently and is worth reading before publishing.
**Open question:** whether it belongs under accessories rather than
cameras, since it is an attachment system.

**Bolex SP8 / SP80** — projectors, absent from this site. Pacific Rim holds
"Bolex SP8/SP80 Instruction Book ME 6-005/75", so c. 1975.
**Confidence:** unconfirmed beyond the existence of the document.

**Bolex 5120** — named in a Pacific Rim "Dealer Notebook Page". Nothing
else known; may be a projector, may be an accessory.
**Confidence:** unconfirmed. Do not publish on a filename alone.

## Also raised, still unverified

**An H-9 projector.** Not found in any source read so far. The H-9 *camera*
is in the catalogue (and is the one entry with no photograph). The name
suggests 9.5mm, which makes pathefilm.uk the sensible place to look.
**Confidence:** unconfirmed — see `h9.md`.

## Worth having, not a gap

The *Bolex Reporter* runs 1967–1972 at Pacific Rim. This site already has a
Bolex Reporter section under ephemera; these are specific issues that may
or may not be the ones held. Worth diffing issue by issue.

Twenty-nine technical bulletins, 1959–1962, are service material rather
than models — the natural backbone of a repair section.
