The H-9 is the one camera in the catalogue with no imagery at all — its index
entry shows an empty tile. A separate claim is that an H-9 *projector* also
existed, which the archive does not cover.

## What we think is true

- The H-9 camera page was recovered without usable images.
  **Source:** this repository — `getCameraRecords()` reports 53 of 54 models
  with at least one image; the H-9 is the exception.
  **Confidence:** certain.

- An H-9 projector existed.
  **Source:** not yet established.
  **Confidence:** unconfirmed — do not publish until sourced.

## What we still need

- A photograph of the H-9 camera that may be used, with its origin.
- Confirmation that an H-9 projector existed, what gauge it ran, and when.
  9.5mm is the obvious suspect given the name, which makes pathefilm.uk a
  sensible first place to look.
