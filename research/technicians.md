# Technicians: the working register

Everyone we know of, listed or not, and exactly what is known about
each. The published directory is in `lib/technicians.ts`; this is the
file behind it, including the people who are **not** on the site and
why.

Kept because the same names keep resurfacing from different
directions, and without this the same checking gets done twice.

**Rule for the closed group.** The Bolex Camera Users group on
Facebook is the best source there is, and it is closed. Nothing from
it is quoted, attributed or linked on the public site, and no
member's name, email or words appear there. It is used to find out
who exists; each listing is then verified somewhere a stranger could
check for themselves.

Last worked through: 2026-11-23.

## Known of, not on the site

### Robert M. Ditto — United States, town unknown
Plainly real and active: fourteen years of technical posting on
cinematography.com signing himself a Bolex repair tech, an
"Economical Bolex Repair" service advertised there in 2011 at $200,
sells fully serviced cameras with a two-year warranty, and is an
admin of the users' group. Has taken over part of Dieter Schaefer's
university work.
**Blocked on:** no location of any kind, which a map needs. No
current public page; the 2011 advert is fourteen years old. Email
only seen inside the closed group.

### Yves Bornand — Switzerland
Former technician of the original Bolex company, in the S16
conversion department. Named by several people, and one group member
says he reworked a job another technician had botched. A camera
advertised for sale was rebuilt and converted by him.
**Blocked on:** he publishes nothing. His contact is passed privately
between group members and is not ours to publish. Someone would have
to ask him whether he wants listing at all.

### Back to Super 8 — France
Suggested in the group as doing 16mm maintenance. The group's own
list maintainer then checked their site and Instagram and found
nothing about service or CLA: they appear to sell cameras and
cartridges and send film to a lab in Berlin.
**Treat as a dead end** unless someone reports otherwise.

### Camera GmbH — Dortmund, Germany
Appears on the ausgeknipst.de directory of analogue repair workshops
as an active official service partner for various brands. **No Bolex
confirmation anywhere.**

### Filmtechnik E. Piehler — Nauheim, Germany
Listed for camera and projector repair on an old directory. Not
checked against anything of their own.

### P+S Technik — Munich
Suggested in the group by someone who said themselves they did not
know whether it handles Bolex.

### Bolex International — Switzerland
The manufacturer, and the obvious first thought from Europe. Its own
site has **no repair or service page at all**, only a contact form,
and does not even give a town. A group member asked in 2026 whether
they are still in business, saying they answer no messages and
publish no phone number. A 2020 forum post says they were "crazy
busy" and not returning calls. Otello Diotallevi, their technician,
is on the group's retired list.
**Nothing to quote, so nothing to list.**

### ausgeknipst.de — read, and it yields nobody new
A German directory of analogue repair workshops. Not a technician: it
was on the list as a possible source of further names. Read properly on
25 November 2026, and the answer is no.

It is a real and largely maintained list, 319 workshops across 34
countries, with notes dated into November 2026 beside a long tail of
stale entries. But it is a **stills** directory almost end to end.
Around six entries touch cine work at all, and of those:

- **Bolex International** is the only entry that names Bolex, as
  "offizieller Werksservice". Already ruled out above: the company's own
  site has no service page, no town and answers nobody.
- **Film-Mechanik** is Simon Wyss, already listed here.
- **Camera Services Bogner** (Germany) is marked retired, and did Arri
  and Aaton rather than Bolex.
- **Guy Merzbarn** (Berlin) is Braun Nizo Super 8 only.
- **J. A. Coelho da Silva** (Lisbon) lists "Laufbildkameras" among much
  else, with nothing said about Bolex.
- **Hansom's Repairs** (Matthews, North Carolina) looked the most
  promising: a cine entry with a community note dated 10 November 2026.
  Their own site was then read, and it **never mentions Bolex, Paillard,
  8mm, Super 8 or 16mm**. It says "cinema camera repairs tailored for
  filmmakers", which undefined almost certainly means modern digital
  cinema bodies, and every brand it does name is a stills brand. Not
  listable on this evidence. If someone reports a Bolex they actually
  serviced, that changes.

Paillard is not mentioned anywhere on the directory.

**Conclusion: closed as a source.** Worth one more look in a year, since
it is maintained, but it is not where the European Bolex people are.

## Removed, and why

- **Les Bosher** (South Wales) — died 5 December 2025. Two sources: a
  customer on r/cinematography and a "Les Bosher RIP" thread opened
  by his son. His site is still up and unchanged. His son Chris
  worked with him and may continue; unestablished.
- **Dr Bolex** (Tijeras, New Mexico) — removed 23 November on two
  independent accounts of money taken and work not delivered: a flat
  "scammer" on r/16mm in February 2023, and a documented group post
  with invoice and email screenshots, an EBM conversion paid for in
  March 2022 and undelivered a year later. Nothing about the quality
  of the work when it does arrive.
- **Sendean Cameras** (London) — listed and removed the same day. The
  r/bolex endorsement it rested on was withdrawn by its own author,
  who later described what they did to his Bolex EL for £2,000. Two
  others call their cine work vastly overpriced.
- **Martin Stent** (UK) — removed earlier for publishing no contact
  details. Since confirmed retired two years ago on health grounds.
- **Super 16 Inc** (Bernie O'Doherty) — `super16inc.com` is now a
  parked domain for sale. On the group's retired list.
