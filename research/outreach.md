# Outreach

Drafts for asking people directly. Nothing here has been sent — these are
for Andrew to send, edit or discard.

The reasoning: the strongest position for this project is not a careful
reading of what we may take without asking. It is having asked. Most of
the people whose knowledge is being preserved here are alive, reachable,
and would probably be glad someone is doing it. A short honest note turns
a grey area into a collaboration, and sometimes into new material — people
who are asked tend to offer more than was requested.

Standing rule while nothing has been answered: **no permission is claimed
anywhere on the site.** Same as with Michael's work.

---

## 1. The forum technicians

To Dom Jaeger, Simon Wyss, Jean-Louis Seguin and others whose posts would
be quoted. They can be reached through cinematography.com itself, or in
Jean-Louis Seguin's case through his repair business.

> Subject: Preserving the cinematography.com Bolex forum
>
> Hello,
>
> I run bolexcollector.org, which is keeping Michael Tisdale's
> BolexCollector.com online while the original is down.
>
> I've been looking at the Bolex forum on cinematography.com, and the
> reason I'm writing is that it's disappearing. The live site now blocks
> automated access, and of the 571 threads I can find listed, only 326
> still exist in the Internet Archive. The rest are gone — title only.
> Your posts are a large part of what's left: by my count you're among
> the most prolific contributors there, and the threads where you explain
> how these cameras actually work are the most useful Bolex material
> anywhere on the internet.
>
> I'd like to make that searchable, so someone with a sticking H16 can
> type their problem and find the answer you already gave. What I have in
> mind is short quotations, always with your name, the date, and a link
> back to the original thread — a citation, not a copy.
>
> I wanted to ask rather than just do it. Two questions:
>
> 1. Are you happy to be quoted that way?
> 2. Is there anything of yours you'd rather not see reproduced, or
>    anything you've since corrected and wouldn't want repeated?
>
> And if you have material of your own — notes, manuals, photographs —
> that you'd like kept somewhere permanent, I'd be glad to host it and
> credit it properly.
>
> Thanks either way, and thanks for everything you've already written.
